## 🚀 Featured projects

### 🧑‍🍳 [lethimcook](https://github.com/quantum-vaibhav/lethimcook)
**Your AI is cooking. This is the kitchen soundtrack.**
Hooks into Claude Code's lifecycle and plays theme music while it thinks. The music pauses the instant it finishes or needs you, then resumes from the exact same spot. One-click setup on Windows, macOS and Linux.
`Python` `pygame` `Claude Code hooks`

### 📝 [afterthought](https://github.com/quantum-vaibhav/afterthought)
**Ask follow-ups without derailing the conversation.**
A Chrome extension: select any text in a ChatGPT answer, attach a note or question, and optionally get a short AI answer pinned inline. Notes are colour-coded highlights with a searchable sidebar, and they export to Markdown, Anki, or JSON.
`JavaScript` `Chrome MV3` `DOM automation`

### 📚 [booksPalace](https://github.com/quantum-vaibhav/booksPalace)
**Every book I've read, on one shelf.**
Scroll sideways through genres while risograph-style canvas posters morph from one genre into the next. Click a spine and the book turns, opens, and shows what it's about and what I thought of it.
`React` `TypeScript` `Vite` `Canvas` `Motion`
